Undergraduate Thesis · BRAC University Summer 2025 – Spring 2026

Evaluating and Improving Adversarial Robustness in Bangla Sentiment Analysis: A Cross-Architecture Study with Human-Validated Perturbation Taxonomy

Standard sentiment benchmarks overestimate model reliability on morphologically rich, low-resource languages. This study constructs a rigorous, human-validated perturbation taxonomy for Bangla to systematically stress-test and improve cross-architecture model resilience.

46,000+ Annotated Bangla evaluation samples curated & verified
15 Models Classical ML, Transformer & LLM architectures benchmarked
Taxonomy Human-validated linguistic perturbation framework
  • Linguistic Perturbation Analysis: Investigated the vulnerability of Bangla sentiment classifiers against character-level, phonetic, orthographic, and semantic adversarial variations.
  • Corpus Construction & Taxonomy Design: Built and annotated a 46,000+ sample dataset alongside a structured, human-validated perturbation taxonomy tailored to real-world Bangla text.
  • Cross-Architecture Empirical Evaluation: Benchmarked 15 distinct ML, Transformer, and LLM models using Python, PyTorch, and Hugging Face Transformers under controlled adversarial stress tests.
